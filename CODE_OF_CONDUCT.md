# Code of Conduct

Selah is built by people of different churches, traditions, countries and languages. We want this to be
a place where everyone can contribute in peace.

## Our commitments

- **Be kind and patient.** Assume good intent. Many contributors are new to open source or writing in a
  second language.
- **Respect traditions.** Theological differences are real; discuss them graciously and keep the product
  within historic, mainstream Christian teaching (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). Don't use
  issues or pull requests to argue doctrine or to attack another tradition.
- **Protect people.** Never share someone's private messages, prayers or personal stories from the app,
  in issues or anywhere else. Use made-up examples when reporting problems.
- **No harassment.** Insults, discrimination, sexual language, threats, or personal attacks are not
  welcome in any project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who break this code.
To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
